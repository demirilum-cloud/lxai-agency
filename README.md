# LxAI Brand Launch

Build a professional, premium, minimal marketing website for "LxAI" (lowercase x), an AI automation & digital agency based in Prizren, Kosovo, serving Kosovo, North Macedonia, Albania, Germany, Switzerland, and the UK.

BRAND
- Wordmark logo: "LxAI" (lowercase x), bold condensed sans-serif, medium gray on black background, no icon/symbol
- Colors: black and gray only, taken from the logo (use a black/near-black background with a light gray or off-white text, and mid-gray accents — avoid any other accent color)
- Personality: premium, modern, minimal, professional, trustworthy, innovative — "luxury without being flashy"
- Typography: bold condensed sans-serif for headings (something like Archivo, Inter Tight, or similar condensed grotesk), clean and highly legible body font
- Overall feel: dark, sophisticated, lots of negative space, subtle motion/hover states, no clutter, no cheesy stock-photo AI clichés (no glowing brains/robots)

STRUCTURE / SECTIONS
1. Sticky header with LxAI wordmark + nav (Services, About, Process, Pricing, Contact) + a "Book a Call" CTA button
2. Hero section: bold headline about helping businesses automate & grow with AI, subheadline, primary CTA ("Book a Free Consultation") + secondary CTA ("See Our Services"), clean dark background
3. Services grid/section — 10 services, each with a short one-line description:
   - AI Chatbots
   - AI Voice Agents
   - AI Automation / Workflow Automation
   - Custom Websites
   - Landing Pages
   - Appointment Booking Systems
   - Ordering Systems for Restaurants (menu + order notifications, no online payment)
   - CRM Integrations
   - Social Media Management
   - AI Consulting
4. "Who We Help" section — industries: restaurants, dental/medical clinics, gyms, salons, hotels, real estate agencies, e-commerce stores, local service businesses
5. "Our Process" — simple 3-4 step process (Discover → Design → Build/Automate → Launch & Support)
6. Why LxAI / value props section (premium quality, fast turnaround, local + international reach, ongoing support)
7. Testimonials/social proof placeholder section (use tasteful placeholder quotes, clearly generic, no fake specific names/companies presented as real)
8. Pricing / "Get a Quote" section — since services vary widely, use a "starting from" style or a simple contact-for-quote CTA rather than fixed complex pricing tables
9. FAQ section (a few relevant questions about AI automation, timelines, support)
10. Contact / Booking section — a contact form (name, email, business type, message) and space for a booking widget/calendar embed placeholder
11. Footer — LxAI wordmark, nav links, contact info (Prizren, Kosovo), social links, copyright

REQUIREMENTS
- Fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop)
- Smooth scroll and subtle scroll-reveal animations on sections
- Working, functional contact form (client-side validation; can store submissions or show success state — no real backend integration needed unless trivial)
- Fast, clean, production-quality code — this should look like a real premium agency site, not a template demo
- SEO-friendly semantic HTML, proper meta title/description mentioning AI automation agency in Kosovo
- Favicon using the LxAI wordmark style
